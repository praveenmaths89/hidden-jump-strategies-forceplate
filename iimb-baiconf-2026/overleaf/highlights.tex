\noindent\textbf{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
\item Equal jump heights did not imply similar force--time curves in 805 football jumps
\item Four components explained 74.8\% of variance, located mainly in the landing
\item Vendor metrics, age and body mass explained only 2.7--13.1\% of each component
\item Curve-shape differences arose mostly within athletes, not between them
\item Landings already recorded in every test hold information take-off metrics miss
\end{itemize}
