# Data terms

The files under `data/` and the derived outputs under `outputs/` (de-identified countermovement-jump curves and vendor
metrics, component scores, output tables and figures) are derived from VALD ForceDecks recordings of male football
players tested at a high-performance centre in Thane, Mumbai. They are **not** licensed under MIT.

- They are shared only for peer review and for reproducing the analyses in this repository.
- Do not redistribute them, combine them with other data to identify athletes, or attempt re-identification.
  Many of the athletes were under 18 years of age when tested.

  raw exports, which are not included here.
